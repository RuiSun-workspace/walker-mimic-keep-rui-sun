## What the test proves headlessly
- **A1 (trigger):**
  - Nothing is created on scene load.
  - Bursts created = `hit_taken` count = arrows that ended on a hit (7 = 7 = 7), and every fired arrow is accounted for.
  - Each hit creates exactly one burst, in the same frame.
  - An arrow that leaves the screen creates no hit and no burst.
  - **Your change 1:** a real burn-out (one arrow, then real D-key movement out of the cone) creates no burst in the `burning_ended` frame, during the burn, or 10 frames after.
  - The R reset creates no burst.
- **A2 (position):** each burst's `global_position` equals the signal position within 1 px, the signal position equals the scarecrow's centre, and every burst's parent is `World/Effects`.
- **A3 (overlap):**
  - Two hits landed 4 frames apart, inside one lifetime.
  - Three different instances were alive at the third impact.
  - No hit freed, cleared or stopped an earlier burst: each earlier burst was still in the tree with `Straw.emitting` true.
  - Every lifetime was within tolerance, so no burst was restarted or cut short.
- **A4 (lethal hit):** the scarecrow is freed in the hit frame and its burst outlives it for its full lifetime.
- **A5 (cleanup and reset):** nothing finished stays in `Effects`, and after R with a live burst `Effects` is empty within 72 frames (1.20 s).

## What it cannot prove headlessly
