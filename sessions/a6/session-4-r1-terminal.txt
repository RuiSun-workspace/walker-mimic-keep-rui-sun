Why the body barely changed

The tinted body leaves almost no room to brighten. The flash adds light on top of what is already there. The burning body is about (247, 193, 58) in RGB, so red has only 8 levels left before it hits the maximum of 255, and green has 62. In r0 most of the flash over the body already exceeded that and was cut off at 255. Cutting the flash's alpha by about 40% removed mostly brightness that was being cut off anyway, so those pixels stay saturated yellow. Grass has much more room (red is about 106), so the cut shows there. My prediction assumed the dimming would show evenly on every background; it only does where nothing clips.

The chips were never covered by the flash. The Straw emitter comes after Flash in the scene, so it draws on top, with normal (Mix) blending. In frame 12 what hides the chips is their tan colour (#e8c46b) against the similar body colour (#f7c13a), not the flash. This is the risk I raised before the change, and r1 confirms it.

What this suggests

Lowering the flash alpha further would mostly dim the halo over the grass. The blob over the body would stay saturated until the alpha is very low. If the goal is still to see the straw fly out in the first frames, the setting to try next round is probably the Straw colour ramp, with a darker or more contrasting first stop. Another option is a smaller peak in the Flash scale curve, which would make the blob smaller rather than dimmer.
